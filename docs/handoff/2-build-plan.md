# 🏗️ The Build Plan
### Your own all-in-one platform. Two workspaces. No monthly ceilings.

---

## 🎯 The one-line goal

Everything under your roof — **website, blog, email, funnels, social, community, courses** — split into **two separate workspaces** (Serena Gasparini + FemNEST), that you own outright.

**The thread that ties it together:** content → audience → payment. No paid tool does that. You will.

---

## 🧱 The core idea (read this once, then it's simple)

**One spine. Many front doors.**

Every part of the platform writes to the same place: the contact database. Whoever someone is, whatever door they came through — one record, one workspace.

```
     WORKSPACE SWITCHER  (click between the two)
              │
      ┌───────┴────────┐
   SERENA           FEMNEST
   own everything    own everything
      │                 │
   ┌──┴──────────────────┴──┐
   │   THE SPINE (contacts)  │  ← every door writes here
   └──┬──────────────────┬──┘
      │                  │
  Website  Blog  Email  Funnels  Social  Community  Courses
```

## ✅ ALREADY BUILT — Workspace switcher + spine

**What it does:**
- Two workspaces, click between them (like Notion)
- Each has its own contacts, own colour, own world
- Add / view / filter / tag contacts
- Payment-aware: lead → subscriber → paid, with € value

**What's left to make it real:** Stage 1 below.

---

## 1️⃣ STAGE 1 — Make it real (database + hosting)

Right now it's a prototype — refresh wipes it. This stage gives it a memory.

**The 3 things needed (all free to start):**

| Need | Tool | Cost |
|------|------|------|
| 🗄️ Database | Supabase | Free to ~50k contacts |
| 🌐 Hosting | Vercel | Free |
| 💳 Payments | Stripe | Free — % per sale only |

**Go-live cost: €0/month** until real money moves. 🎉

**Done when:** you add a contact, refresh, and it's still there — at your own URL.

---

## 2️⃣ STAGE 2 — Website + Blog + Lead magnets

Now the "content → audience" half.

- 📄 Simple site per workspace (own brand each)
- ✍️ Blog — owns your SEO, no platform rent
- 🧲 Lead magnet → auto-drops the person into that workspace's spine

**Replaces:** paid website builder
**Done when:** someone downloads a freebie and appears in your contacts automatically.

---

## 3️⃣ STAGE 3 — Email + Funnels

The "audience → payment" engine.

- 📧 Email sending (Resend API — near-free)
- 🔁 Sequences: welcome, nurture (hard-coded, not a fiddly builder)
- 🪝 Funnel = landing page → capture → sequence → offer

**Replaces:** Kit
**Switch only when proven.** Keep Kit running in parallel first.

---

## 4️⃣ STAGE 4 — Social builder (the AI one) 🤖

Topic in → post out.

```
1. 📝 Type a topic
2. 🤖 AI writes caption in THAT workspace's voice
3. 🎨 AI renders the brand graphic
4. 👀 Approval gate — sign-off required  ← non-negotiable
5. 📅 Slots into calendar
6. 🚀 Auto-posts to IG / LinkedIn / Threads
```

**Replaces:** Buffer
**Developer flag 🚩:** steps 1–5 build as a full working prototype. Step 6 (auto-post) needs the real Meta/LinkedIn APIs — only works once deployed. So: build 1–5 now, wire 6 at go-live.

## 7️⃣ STAGE 7 — Team access

Your small team, safely.

- 👥 Invite people per workspace

---

## 🗺️ Suggested order (why this sequence)

1. **Make it real first** (Stage 1) — no point building on sand
2. **Website + email next** (2–3) — the money-making core
3. **Social builder** (4) — your biggest daily time-saver
4. **Community + courses** (5–6) — the heavy builds, last
5. **Team** (7) — bring people in once it's stable

---

## ⚖️ The honest trade-off (keep this in view)

**You gain:** no monthly fees · no feature ceilings · two clean brands · you own the data.

**It costs:** this is ~7 big products combined. Built one stage at a time it's very doable. Built all at once it's a mess. **Patience is the strategy.**
